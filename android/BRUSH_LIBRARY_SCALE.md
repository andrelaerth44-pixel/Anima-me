# Anima-me Brush Library

The catalog is intentionally generated from original procedural presets and parameterized variants.

- 90+ seed brushes across drawing, ink, paint, watercolor, marker, effects, nature, texture and utility families.
- 9 behavioral variants per seed: Fine, Soft, Hard, Pressure, Dense, Dry, Wet, Textured, Fine Grain.
- The resulting catalog is above 800 presets and remains scalable without storing hundreds of duplicate bitmap assets.
- Each preset receives independent `BrushSettings` defaults and can later be replaced by authored raster/vector/texture assets.
- Proprietary third-party brush files are not bundled.
